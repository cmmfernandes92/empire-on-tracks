## How the work was organised

The project ran over three sprints following a Scrum-inspired process. Global
artefacts (glossary, use case diagram, supplementary specification and domain
model) were produced and maintained by the whole team, and the User Stories
were distributed among the members each sprint. Integration of the User Stories
into a coherent system was a shared, team-wide responsibility, and every member
was expected to understand the project as a whole rather than only their own
part.
